## 2. Background & Societal Context

In many state and local government bodies in India, citizen feedback and civic complaints are gathered via traditional grievance portals or municipal hotlines. However, when civic complaints escalate into innovation calls or university research projects, four key structural breakdown points occur:

### Breakdown 1: Absence of Problem Qualification
Without a standardized qualification gate, trivial service defects (such as a broken water pipe or missing garbage bin) are submitted as R&D innovation proposals. This misallocates research capital and delays routine civic repairs.

### Breakdown 2: Lack of Verifiable Capability Matching
Institutions are often selected based on general prestige rather than specific, verified technical capabilities (e.g., environmental sensor labs, water testing facilities). This results in mismatched partnerships and non-viable pilot designs.

### Breakdown 3: Fictional Readiness & Silent Invalidation
A pilot project is often declared "ready for launch" based on initial enthusiasm. When a partner institution later withdraws key equipment or financial backing, the project state remains listed as "Active/Ready" in administrative systems, wasting field execution budgets.

### Breakdown 4: Outcome Conflation
At the end of a pilot execution period, municipal reports often treat the expiration of the timeline (`COMPLETED`) as proof of technical and societal success. Without independent evidence plan baselines and denominator checks, ineffective solutions are scaled to production.

## 3. Scope & Objectives of NIRNAY

NIRNAY was designed to provide a digital governance framework that enforces:

1. **Structured Civic Challenge Passports**: Standardized problem reporting with evidence attachments, spatial coordinates, and domain tags.
2. **Authoritative Human Gatekeeping**: Qualification decisions backed by verifiable rationale and multi-tier review capabilities.
3. **Formal Institutional Commitments**: Versioned commitment tracking for HEI and Industry resource allocation.
4. **Automated State Dependency Verification**: Algorithmic recalculation of readiness whenever underlying conditions change.
5. **Separated Outcome Integrity Assessment**: Explicit structural separation between operational activity completion and evidence-backed impact validation.
6. **Transparent Audit Trails**: Full decision assurance recording for governance accountability.
